Know before you go

A practical guide to the hills.

Simple planning notes for a smoother Darjeeling and Sikkim journey. Confirm route-specific requirements when you call.

Pace

Plan by road, not only distance.

Mountain kilometres take longer than they look. Leave breathing room for curves, town traffic, tea stops and changing weather.

  • Start early for popular viewpoints
  • Keep transfer days lighter
  • Build in meal and photo stops

Respect

Travel gently in sacred places.

Monasteries are active places of worship. Follow local signs, ask before photographing people and keep voices low around prayer halls.

  • Dress for cool interiors
  • Follow photography rules
  • Make time to observe quietly
Sikkim note: Some routes can involve permits or identity-document requirements. Confirm the current requirements for your nationality and chosen route before travel.

More useful notes

The details that shape a good hill trip.

A little preparation makes winding roads, cool mornings and changing weather much easier to enjoy.

What to pack

Dress in layers

Mornings and evenings can feel much cooler than the plains, even when afternoons are bright.

  • Light warm layer
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Rain protection in wet months

On the road

Travel light where possible

Compact luggage is easier to load on hill journeys and leaves more room inside the vehicle.

  • Keep essentials accessible
  • Carry water and simple snacks
  • Tell us about bulky bags early

With family

Make the pace realistic

Shorter driving blocks and planned breaks can make mountain travel more comfortable for children and older guests.

  • Share mobility needs
  • Allow rest after arrival
  • Avoid packing every day full

After the viewpoints

Leave room for town life.

The hills are more than scenic stops. Markets, evening streets, food and small conversations often become the parts travellers remember most.
